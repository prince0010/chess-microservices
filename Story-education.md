# Puzzle Story gameplay :

- Level 1
  . Pawn (20 puzzles)
  . King (20 puzzles)
  . Bishop (20 puzzles)
  . Knight (20 puzzles)
  . Rook (20 puzzles)
  . Queen (20 puzzles)

- Level 2 (250 puzzles)
- Level 3 - Mate in One (250 puzzles)

## Timer

- 30 seconds per puzzle

## Quantity of completed to unlock disables - Points

. Level 1: Solve at least 10 puzzles to get to Test Level. 1 point per puzzle.
. Level 2: Solve at least 50 puzzles to get to Test Level. 2 point per puzzle
. Level 3: Solve at least 50 puzzles to get to Test Level. 2 point per puzzle

## Test

- Score at least 7/10. to complete the Test
