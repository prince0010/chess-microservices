# What happens when student fail 3 times a puzzle - Rule:

- This rule says that player needs to wait 5 minutes to be able to open and play puzzle.
- This rule only apply to lessons-puzzles, not apply to 4 games or animal bots.

# My Pet - function Rule:

- give extra life.
- give extra 60 seconds time.

. These extra Lives - Times can be spent playing puzzles.

# My Pet - actions

- Panda eat
- Panda sleep

# Panda help unblock story

- when student fails any puzzle (not a test) that story automatically is blocked. Here if player tap on Unblock timer 5 minutes countdown widget Panda jump and offers help for 30 Panda Points.

# Panda help continue to next puzzle

- when student fails some puzzle and lost the 3 lives, panda jump and help player to be able to continue to next puzzle for 15 Panda Points.
