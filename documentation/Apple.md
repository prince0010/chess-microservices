# Apple instruction to get started.

## Steps

**Step_1** Check if You Have an Apple ID
If you already have an Apple ID (you use an iPhone, Mac, or have ever downloaded from the App Store), skip to **Step_2**

1. If you don't have an Apple ID:
2. Go to [appleid.apple.com](https://account.apple.com/)
3. Click "Create Your Apple ID"
4. Fill in your information using your business email address
5. Verify your email address

**Step_2** Get Your D-U-N-S Number (Businesses Only if you have a company)
What is a D-U-N-S Number?

- It's a unique 9-digit number for businesses
- It's free and usually takes 1-2 business days
- Apple requires this to verify your business

How to get it:

1. Go to dnb.com/duns-number
2. Click "Get a D-U-N-S Number"
3. Fill in your business information
4. Wait for the number via email (usually 1-2 days)

   Note: If you're an individual/sole proprietor, you may not need this.

**Step_3** Enroll in Apple Developer Program

1. Go to the enrollment page: [developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/)
2. Click "Start Your Enrollment"
3. Sign in with your Apple ID
4. Select entity type:

- Organization/Company (if you have a business)
- Individual/Sole Proprietor (if you're an individual)

5. Fill in your information:

- Legal name (exactly as it appears on official documents)
- Address
- Phone number
- D-U-N-S Number (if you're a business)

6. Complete the purchase:

- Pay the $99 USD annual fee
- This is a recurring yearly subscription

**Step_4** Wait for Verification (1-3 Business Days)
