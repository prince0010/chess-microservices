We Chess Privacy Policy

Protecting parents, children, and young chess learners

Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how We Chess collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use our website, platform services, mobile application, or when parents purchase coaching lessons for their children. By accessing or using We Chess, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Introduction

We Chess is an online learning and entertainment platform focused on chess education, interactive puzzles, gameplay with bots, and a virtual pet system. Our website additionally allows parents or guardians to purchase online chess lessons for their children. This Privacy Policy applies to all users, including parents, guardians, and children using the service under authorized supervision.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: Username, email address, or login credentials.
  • Child Account Information: Name or nickname, age group, or parent-linked identifier.
  • Payment Information: Processed securely by Stripe. We Chess does not store credit card numbers.
  • Support Requests: Messages sent to our support email.

2.2 Information Automatically Collected

  • Device and Browser Information: OS, browser version.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, gameplay actions.
  • Cookies and Tracking: Session and analytics cookies.
  • Security Data: IP address and logs.

2.3 Information About Children

We Chess collects limited child data only when created by a parent or guardian and only for educational purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage user accounts.
  • To provide lessons, puzzles, and gameplay.
  • To process payments through Stripe.
  • To track child learning progress.
  • To improve performance and security.

4. Purchases Made by Parents for Children

  • Parents manage child accounts.
  • Progress is stored for learning continuity.
  • No unnecessary child data is collected.
  • Payments handled by Stripe.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Consent
  • Contract
  • Legitimate interest
  • Parental authorization

6. Data Sharing

  • Stripe (payments)
  • Analytics services
  • Coaches (limited progress data)

7. Children’s Privacy

  • No child payments
  • No targeted ads
  • Parents can request deletion

8. Data Security

We use encryption, secure servers, and monitoring systems to protect data.

9. Data Storage & Retention

  • Stored until deletion request
  • Progress stored while active
  • Purchase data stored for legal reasons

10. Your Rights

  • Access
  • Correction
  • Export
  • Deletion
  • Consent revocation

11. Cookies

Essential and analytics cookies are used. You may disable non-essential cookies.

12. Third-Party Links

We are not responsible for third-party privacy practices.

13. Updates to This Privacy Policy

Updates will be posted on this page with a new date.
